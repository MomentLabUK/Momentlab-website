The Spotlight Experience

A single, striking studio portrait

The Spotlight Experience strips everything back to the essentials. A deep obsidian background, one considered light, and your guest at the centre of it. No clutter, no distraction, just a portrait with real presence.

It is the most restrained of our experiences, and often the most memorable. The kind of frame people keep on the mantelpiece, not the fridge.

Why it works

Restraint is the whole point

Most booths add. The Spotlight Experience takes away. A single directional light does the work of a full studio, shaping the face and giving every portrait a quiet, editorial drama.

The result flatters everyone and dates well, made in seconds and printed to keep. Understated, and all the stronger for it.

Questions

Spotlight photo booth hire, answered

Can you brand the Spotlight portraits to our event? +

Yes. The backdrop, the print design and the on-screen welcome can all be shaped around your names, your date or your brand.

How much does photo booth hire cost in Newcastle and the North East? +

Pricing depends on the length of your hire, your guest numbers and the experiences you choose, so every proposal is bespoke. Tell us about your occasion and we will send a tailored quote.

Which areas do you cover? +

Newcastle and the wider North East, including Gateshead, Jesmond, Gosforth, Whitley Bay, Tynemouth, North Shields, Sunderland, Durham, Tyne and Wear and Northumberland. Share your venue and we will confirm.

Do you charge for travel across the North East? +

Newcastle and the wider North East are covered as standard, with no surprise travel charges. For venues further afield we are happy to travel, and we will confirm any mileage when we quote.

How much space and power does the booth need? +

Roughly two metres by two metres of floor space and a standard plug socket nearby. If your venue is tighter, tell us and we will usually work something out.

How many guests can use the booth at once? +

Our open-air setup is built for groups, so there is no squeezing into a box. The more the better, and everyone in the frame can take home their own keepsake.

Is there an attendant on the day? +

Yes. Every booking includes a dedicated Moment Lab attendant on site throughout, arriving around 60 to 90 minutes before your booth opens to set up and test everything before your guests arrive.

How do I secure my date? +

Once you are happy with your bespoke proposal, your date is held with a deposit and a signed agreement. Until then, dates are first come, first served, so it is worth enquiring early.

Do guests receive their photos and video straight away? +

Yes. Prints are ready on the night, and the digital photos and any video clips reach guests by QR code, ready to share. The host receives the full gallery in full resolution after the event.
